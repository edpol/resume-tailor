**The 6 Résumé Secrets:**  

1. **Hire for Safety:** Avoid vague, buzzword-heavy bullet points. Instead, use clear, result-oriented language that proves you have already performed the tasks required for the role (1:36-2:17).  

2. **Explain Risk Upfront:** Don't leave gaps, pivots, or short job stints unexplained. Provide a brief, neutral, one-line context for these items so recruiters don't assume the worst (2:27-4:07).  

3. **Translate Your Experience:** Tailor your bullet points to match the language and business priorities found in the job description (4:18-5:23).  

4. **Use Strong Verbs:** Use leadership-oriented verbs like led, spearheaded, or drove to convey seniority, rather than passive terms like supported or helped (5:46-6:59).  

5. **Focus on Recent Impact:** Your résumé is a signal, not a life story. Prioritize the last 5–7 years of your experience to keep your narrative sharp and relevant (7:12-8:23).  

6. **Show Balanced Range:** Effective résumés balance three pillars: technical credibility, business impact, and leadership (8:25-9:23).
