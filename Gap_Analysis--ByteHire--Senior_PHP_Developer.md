## Priority Gaps

### 1. PostgreSQL-Specific Experience [MEDIUM PRIORITY]

**What's missing**: JD lists "MySQL, PostgreSQL, or other relational databases" but your experience is primarily MySQL.

**Why it matters**: While relational database concepts transfer directly, PostgreSQL has unique features: JSON/JSONB types, advanced indexing (BRIN, partial indexes), materialized views, and performance tuning differs from MySQL.

**Fix**: In interviews, emphasize your transferable database optimization skills (indexing strategies, query analysis, performance tuning). Explicitly note willingness to learn PostgreSQL-specific patterns. MySQL skills translate well because the fundamentals are identical.
